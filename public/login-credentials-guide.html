EASA COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING AND TECHNOLOGY

Portal Access & Login Credentials Guide

Confidential System Reference Document • Prepared for Project Owner • Date: September 2026

1. Dedicated Administrative Portals

1. Dedicated Admissions & Quick Enquiry Portal
Admissions & Leads
Route / URL: /admin/admission  (or /admin/addmission)
Master Username: admin
Master Password: admin123
Coordinator Accounts: Create new Admission Staff username & password accounts directly inside the "Team Logins & Passwords" tab.
Scope & Features: Unified command center combining Student Admission Registrations and Quick Website Enquiries. Student biodata popup, SSLC/HSC marks, status workflows (Pending, Reviewed, Accepted, Rejected), direct 1-click call/email shortcuts, manual entry for offline walk-ins, and Excel (.xlsx) export.
2. Master Admin CMS Dashboard
Super Admin
Route / URL: /admin/login  or  /admin
Master Username: admin
Master Password: admin123
Scope & Features: Full CMS control: Edit homepage hero slides, flash news/events, faculty statistics, research publications, photo/video gallery, and admin account management.
3. Syllabus & Academic Management System
Role-Based Auth
Route / URL: /login (Redirects to /dashboard on success)
Supported Roles: Admin, HOD, Staff / Faculty, Student
Master Login: Username: admin   Password: admin123
Scope & Features: Academic curriculum & syllabus builder, Course Outcomes (COs), Program Outcomes (PO/PSO) mapping matrices, Blooms Taxonomy indicators, unit syllabus authoring, and PDF/Word syllabus export.
4. Recruitment & Job Applications Hub
HR & Recruitment
Route / URL: /admin/applications
Login Credentials: Username: admin   Password: admin123
Scope & Features: Post new job vacancies, toggle active/closed status, view candidate applications, interactive PDF resume preview & download, candidate stage workflows (Shortlisted, Interviewed, Hired, Rejected), and Excel export.
5. Institutional Campus ERP (External Portal)
Campus ERP
Official Link: https://portal.easacollege.com/Login
Scope & Features: College enterprise management: Semester examination marks, daily attendance tracking, student fee payments, and institutional records.
