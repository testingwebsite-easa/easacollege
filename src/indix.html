Welcome to EASA Alumni Connect

Welcome to the EASA Alumni Connect community. At EASA College of Engineering and Technology, we believe that our relationship with our students continues long after graduation. Our Alumni serve as ambassadors of the Institution across Engineering, Entrepreneurship, Research, Healthcare, Education, Innovation and Public Service.

The Alumni Connect initiative builds a lifelong relationship between Alumni and the Institution through Networking, Mentorship, Career development and Collaboration.

Our Vision

Our Mission
